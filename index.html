    How to play
    Look at the row of pictures and name each one out loud, in order, the plainest way you can. Then say them again faster and run the sounds together. The spelling of the answer can be quite different from the pictures, so trust your ears rather than your eyes.
    Type your answer and press Guess. You have five tries. After two misses I show you a hint. If you still haven't got it after five, the answer appears with each picture spelled out.
    When you finish you get a result line to share: a red square for each miss, a green one for the solve and a light bulb if you saw the hint. It never gives the answer away.
    

    About rebus puzzles
    A rebus uses pictures, letters or numbers to stand for the sounds of words. They are very old. Coats of arms used them as visual puns on family names, and Victorian puzzle pages were full of them. Plenty of us met them first in comics, on cereal boxes or on telly game shows.
    Say It Slowly sticks to one kind: a row of pictures read left to right purely by sound. No letters crossed out, no words placed inside other words. Some answers are compound words where the pictures are almost the spelling, like starfish. The better ones are where the sound is the only link, like four and tune making fortune.
    All the pictures are standard emoji or simple drawings I made myself, and every answer is checked in a British accent.
    

    FAQ
    Is it the same puzzle for everyone?
    Yes. Everyone gets the same pictures on the same UK date, so you can compare results with friends. A new one goes up at midnight UK time.
    Do I have to spell it exactly?
    Mostly. Capitals, spaces, apostrophes and other punctuation don't matter, so ICE CREAM, icecream and Ice Cream all count. Where a word has a common second spelling I accept that too.
    Why doesn't a picture sound right to me?
    Every puzzle is built to work in a British accent, where we drop most r sounds at the ends of words, so bear foot gives barefoot. If one doesn't work for you, email me and I'll have a look.
    What happens when I run out of tries?
    The answer appears with the pictures spelled out, the day counts as played and your streak goes back to zero. Come back tomorrow for a fresh one.
    Where are my stats kept?
    Only in this browser, under the key sayitslowly:stats. Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing your site data clears them.
    Can I play old puzzles?
    Not yet. It's one a day for now. If enough people ask, I'll add an archive.
